Every game you’ve played, in one list

MyVideoGameList is a video game tracker. It is not open yet: it is being built in the open, and the Discord server is where you will hear when it is.

Join the Discord

What it does

  • Lists by status

    • Backlog
    • Playing
    • On Hold
    • Finished
    • Dropped

    A game sits in one of the five at a time, and you move it as you play.

  • A backlog and a wishlist

    The backlog holds what you mean to play; the wishlist, what you want. A game can be on the wishlist and in a list at once.

  • Scores and reviews

    Your own score, in half stars, and a review you keep to yourself or publish on your public profile and the game’s page.

  • Playthroughs, with hours

    Each run with its dates and hours, played rushed, normally or as a completionist. Members’ runs give each game its typical time to finish.

  • A release calendar for your games

    A year ahead for the games you track: what comes out on which day, and what is known only to its month, quarter or year.

  • Import from other trackers

    Bring a library over with its shelves, dates and playthroughs: Grouvee first, and more trackers to come.

What is coming

  • Email confirmation and password resets, and signing in with Google or Facebook.
  • A search box on every page, suggesting games as you type.
  • Your backlog and your favourites in the order you choose, and changes to many games at once.
  • Profiles with a display name, an avatar and a bio.
  • Later: where to buy a game and for how much, your Steam library, and following other players.

The plan is public: it is the issues of the repository on GitHub, where the code is too.
