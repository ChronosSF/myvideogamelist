What it is
The real MyVideoGameList, at dev.myvideogamelist.net, open for about two weeks while it is tested.
What you keep
The account and the lists you make there move to the real site when it launches.
If it breaks
It is a test, and things may break. When something does, or reads wrong, say so on the Discord or at support@myvideogamelist.net.
Other trackers
It imports a library from Grouvee today. One reason for the test is to collect exports from other trackers to build the importer on, so if you keep your games somewhere else, bring an export from there to the Discord.
Signing up
Signing up asks for an email address, which you confirm from a link sent to it. The privacy policy and the terms are on the test site.

What it keeps for you

What the tracker does today

  1. 01

    Your lists

    Playing, Backlog, On Hold, Finished and Dropped. A game sits in one at a time and moves as you play, and the backlog keeps the order you’ll play it in.

  2. 02

    A wishlist that keeps the dates

    The games you want, and a calendar of when each comes out: to the day, or to the month, quarter or year where that is all that is known.

  3. 03

    Scores and reviews

    Your own score, in half stars, and a review you keep to yourself or publish on your profile and the game’s page.

  4. 04

    Every playthrough

    Each run with its dates and hours, played rushed, normally or as a completionist. Members’ runs give a game its typical time to finish.

  5. 05

    Your year in numbers

    What you’ve finished, and the hours it took.

  6. 06

    Your library from Grouvee

    Bring it across with its shelves, dates and playthroughs, and see every game before anything is saved.
